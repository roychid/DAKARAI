About

Big House Construction

Big House Construction (BHC) is a Southern African construction and development group focused on delivering high-quality residential, commercial, infrastructure and special projects that contribute to the growth of communities and economies across the region.

BHC brings together experienced professionals, specialist businesses and strategic partners to address complex development challenges — from building multiple-unit residential communities and commercial spaces to delivering roads, bridges, water and sanitation infrastructure and other major development initiatives.

With a growing regional presence across Zimbabwe, Botswana, Namibia and Mozambique, BHC combines local knowledge with technical expertise and a commitment to disciplined project execution. The group works with both public and private investors, building lasting partnerships and translating ambitious plans from blueprints to finishings without excuses.

At the heart of BHC is a commitment to quality excellence, sustainability and responsibility, collaboration, innovation and growth, and meaningful community impact. The organisation continues to explore new ways of delivering efficient, durable and responsible developments while creating opportunities for the communities and economies it serves.

Leadership

Chief Executive Officer

Dakarai Albert Mapuranga leads Big House Construction as Chief Executive Officer, with a mandate to drive strategic growth and regional expansion across the company's active markets.

His leadership spans the delivery of residential, commercial and civil engineering projects, alongside the cultivation of strategic partnerships across Southern Africa.

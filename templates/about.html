About

A builder before he was an executive.

Dakarai Albert Mapuranga's path runs through corporate leadership before it runs through construction. His senior experience at The Broadhaven Group shaped how he now leads at Big House Construction — with a focus on delivery, partnership, and the discipline large regional projects demand.

He is a Kutama College alumnus, and his interests have grown beyond infrastructure into agriculture, where he holds a stake in Platinum Group Agriculture. The throughline across both is the same: build things that hold up, and back the people building them.

As CEO of BHC, his mandate is regional — strategic growth across four countries, stronger delivery on residential, commercial and civil engineering work, and the partnerships that make expansion at this scale possible.

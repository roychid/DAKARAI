Dakarai Mapuranga named CEO of Big House Construction

The Herald reports that business executive Mr Dakarai Mapuranga was appointed Chief Executive Officer of Big House Construction (BHC), a regional construction company operating in Botswana, Namibia, Mozambique, and Zimbabwe.

The article highlights his leadership experience at The Broadhaven Group and his mandate to support BHC's growth and its ambition to deliver modern, sustainable, and community-focused construction solutions across Southern Africa.
